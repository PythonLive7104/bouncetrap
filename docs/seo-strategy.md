# BounceTrap SEO Content Strategy

A revenue-focused organic-growth plan for a bootstrapped email-verification SaaS.
Goal: reach **$1K → $5K → $10K MRR** through SEO.

> **Leverage you already have:** Your app already ships SPF/DKIM/DMARC checks, MX health,
> domain reputation monitoring, SMTP testing, and a blog engine. Half the "free tools"
> below are built — they just need public, indexable URLs. Prioritize exposing those.

---

## 0. Strategic Overview — Where the money is

For a bootstrapped SaaS, **commercial-intent pages convert first; content clusters compound later.**

| Phase | MRR target | Focus | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Phase 1 (Mo 1–3)** | First $1K | Comparison + Alternative pages, 5 free tools | Bottom-funnel, buyer-ready traffic. Lowest volume but highest conversion. |
| **Phase 2 (Mo 4–7)** | $1K → $5K | Verification cluster + industry pages | Mid-funnel "how to / best tool for X". Captures problem-aware buyers. |
| **Phase 3 (Mo 8–12)** | $5K → $10K | Deliverability authority cluster + programmatic | Topical authority lifts the whole domain; programmatic scales long-tail. |

**Rule of thumb:** every comparison/alternative/tool page links to **/register** (your 100 free credits offer). Every cluster article links up to a pillar and across to a money page.
